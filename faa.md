## Key Findings

- **Mass FAA Layoffs & Leadership Vacuum:** The administration fired key personnel, leaving air traffic control and regulatory oversight dangerously understaffed.

- **Privatization & Corruption:** Elon Musk has proposed **SpaceX-controlled air traffic systems**, despite his history of **FAA safety violations**.
