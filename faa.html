FAA Cuts & Aviation Safety Crisis

The January 2025 aviation data reveals a troubling rise in fatalities and serious injuries, correlating directly with the dismantling of the FAA under the Musk-Trump administration. This report provides an in-depth analysis of the crisis, supported by data from the National Transportation Safety Board (NTSB).

Key Findings

  • Mass FAA Layoffs & Leadership Vacuum: The administration fired key personnel, leaving air traffic control and regulatory oversight dangerously understaffed.
  • Surging Fatalities: Over 80 of the 87 worldwide aviation fatalities in January 2025 occurred in the U.S., accounting for 92% of global deaths.
  • Privatization & Corruption: Elon Musk has proposed SpaceX-controlled air traffic systems, despite his history of FAA safety violations.

This follows a familiar pattern—sabotage public agencies, create a crisis, then privatize for profit. We must demand restoration of oversight and accountability before more lives are lost.
